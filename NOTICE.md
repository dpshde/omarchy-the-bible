The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with
Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation
Committee. This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain.

Local verse text is fetched at build time from the Arweave transaction:

https://arweave.net/B6yeNb3lk_VkiIp-fTWVh13TlM94LjLK6kC63BPXa8s

## route.bible

User-visible product name is route.bible. This plugin opens canonical
route.bible URLs; it is not a replacement for the website.

## margin.bible

Outline opens `https://margin.bible/{slug}` on the same grab-bcv / OSIS
address as route.bible. Notes live in margin.bible; this plugin only
launches the reader.
